Jonathan James Search (JJS) is a founder-led executive search and talent advisory practice built on more than 20 years of recruiting experience across multiple industries and leadership disciplines.

With extensive expertise in the US construction market, JJS partners with national and regional developers, general contractors, and specialty subcontractors to identify and attract the talent that drives business performance.

We recognize that no two organizations or hiring challenges are the same. Our flexible search solutions are tailored to your specific needs, combining deep industry knowledge, established relationships, and a highly personal approach.

From the initial conversation through to the final appointment, you work directly with the experience and expertise behind JJS. No unnecessary layers. No handoffs. Just informed judgment, personal accountability, and a genuine commitment to making your next hire count.
