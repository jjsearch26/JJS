Jonathan James Search (JJS) is an executive search and talent advisory practice built on more than 20 years of recruiting experience across multiple industries and leadership disciplines.

With established expertise in the US construction market and an expanding presence across specialist industry sectors, JJS brings together experienced search professionals and senior industry practitioners who offer genuine insight into the markets they serve.

Our approach is founded on the belief that effective executive search requires more than access to talent. It demands a thorough understanding of the industry, the challenges facing an organization, and the leadership qualities needed to drive meaningful business performance.

As JJS continues to develop its specialist practices, each sector will be supported by professionals with substantial industry experience, applying the same disciplined search methodology, informed judgment, and relationship-led approach that define our business.

We recognize that no two organizations or leadership challenges are the same. Our flexible search and advisory solutions are tailored to each client's needs, providing direct access to experienced professionals who understand the market, appreciate the complexities of senior appointments, and take personal accountability for delivering the right outcome.
